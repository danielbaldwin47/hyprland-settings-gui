What the two views share
One modelBoth views render the same Schema and the same Rows. A view is a grouping and a naming, nothing more — no option exists in one view only.
One searchSearch is view-independent and always shows the dotted key. Activating a result jumps to whichever page holds it in the current view.
Deep linksThe dotted key is the address. B's page is always the key's own section, so a link, a wiki reference or an error message resolves in both views.
B is generatedB comes from the schema alone — it needs no curation and cannot drift, which is what makes it the safety net for A.
A degrades, never breaksAn unplaced or unknown option shows on its section's page in a New in <version> group, flagged, until someone curates it.
Release checkEach Hyprland release: regenerate the schema, diff against the last, place what is new, retire what is gone. Needs a written procedure — its own ticket.
