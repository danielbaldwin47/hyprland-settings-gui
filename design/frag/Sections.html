353 options in 21 config sections, plus ten kinds of entity that are not options at all. The question is only how they are grouped and named — the row rendering, search and instant apply are identical in all three.

Why: the sidebar is hyprctl descriptions — 21 sections then one page per entity kind. Zero curation, nothing to re-check on upgrade, and the wiki, hyprctl and every forum post name things the same way the app does.
Cost: 29+ items that scroll, a 60-row input page and a 40-row misc junk drawer; the app teaches Hyprland's internal layout rather than hiding it.

Everything else is reached by typing — the search field is focused on launch and matches option names, titles, rules and monitors.
Why: six doors, no sidebar scrolling, and the smallest thing to design and keep coherent. Sub-navigation lives inside the page as a view switcher, the way GNOME Settings' Mouse panel does.
Cost: pages become long and the second level is invisible until you open a door; people who don't search have to scroll a 100-row Appearance page. Discovery leans hard on a search index that still has to be built.
